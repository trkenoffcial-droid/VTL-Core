# VTL Core name and logo

The VTL Core source code is free software under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)).
You may use, study, change and share it, and even sell it, on that license's terms.

The license covers the code, not the brand. Section 2.3 of the MPL says it "does not grant any
rights in the trademarks, service marks, or logos of any Contributor". This page explains how you
may use the Viet TierList names and logos.

## The names and logos

- The names **VTL Core**, **Viet TierList**, **VTL** and **tierslist.net**.
- The VTL logo and wordmark, in `src/main/resources/assets/vtl/icon.png`,
  `src/main/resources/assets/vtl/textures/gui/icon.png` and
  `src/main/resources/assets/vtl/textures/gui/wordmark.png`, and any image that shows them.

## Fine without asking

- Saying what your work is based on: "based on VTL Core", "a fork of VTL Core", "works with
  Viet TierList".
- Writing about VTL Core, reviewing it, showing it in videos, linking to it.
- Sharing an unchanged official release for free, with a link to this repository.

## Not allowed

- Publishing a changed version (a fork, a rebuilt jar, a modpack edit) under the names above or
  with the logos. Give it your own name and your own logo.
- Saying or suggesting that your version is official, or made, checked or endorsed by
  Viet TierList.
- Selling, renting or putting behind a paywall anything that carries the names or logos.
  VTL Core is free.
- Using the names or logos for a project, account, server or shop so that people think it
  belongs to Viet TierList.

## If you publish a fork

1. Change `name` and `id` in `fabric.mod.json`, `archives_base_name` in `gradle.properties`,
   the `vtl.hub.brand` text, and the logo files listed above.
2. Keep `LICENSE`, `NOTICE.md` and every copyright and license notice in the source
   (MPL 2.0, section 3.4).
3. Share the source of the MPL files with every build you give out (MPL 2.0, sections 3.1
   and 3.2).

## Official builds

Official VTL Core builds come only from the Releases page of
[trkenoffcial-droid/VTL-Core](https://github.com/trkenoffcial-droid/VTL-Core/releases) and from
Viet TierList's own channels ([tierslist.net](https://www.tierslist.net) and the Viet TierList
Discord). A build from anywhere else may be changed or unsafe.

## Questions and reports

To ask for any other use, or to report a copy that breaks these rules, contact Viet TierList on
its Discord or open an issue in this repository.
